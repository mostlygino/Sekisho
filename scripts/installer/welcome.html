Sekisho 関所

Stop, read and change every request on its way through. A native Mac debugging proxy for HTTP and HTTPS.

This installs Sekisho __VERSION__ in Applications. Sekisho is free software (AGPL-3.0-or-later); its source is at github.com/mostlygino/Sekisho.

Reading HTTPS needs Sekisho's own certificate authority, made on this Mac at first launch. Sekisho asks before it adds it to your login keychain, and macOS asks for your password. The private key never leaves this Mac.

Sekisho listens on 127.0.0.1:9090 only, and changes the macOS proxy only when you switch it on. Quitting puts the previous setting back.

Needs

  • macOS 26 or later, Apple silicon
