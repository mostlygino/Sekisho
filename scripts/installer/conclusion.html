Sekisho is installed

Open it from Applications or Spotlight.

First steps

  • Click Trust Certificate… at the foot of the sidebar, so HTTPS can be read.
  • Switch on macOS Proxy in the toolbar, or point one app at 127.0.0.1:9090.
  • Right-click a host and choose Enable SSL Proxying to decrypt it.
  • ⌘K finds any request, host, app or action; ⇧⌘T opens Tools: breakpoints, Map Local and Remote, scripts and more.

Just looking?

Try it without touching your network settings or keychain:

open -n /Applications/Sekisho.app --args -demo YES
